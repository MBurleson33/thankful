        How to play Fast Finger Trivia

        Know it? Tap it fast.

        1. Join

        • The host taps Host a new game and reads out the code. Everyone joins on their own phone. You need at least 2 players.
        • The host picks the categories, how many questions (10, 15 or 20) and how long you get for each one.

        2. Answer

        • Each question shows for a few seconds so you can read it, then the four answers appear.
        • Tap the one you think is right. Your first tap locks in.

        3. Score

        • A right answer is worth 500 to 1,000 points. Tap right away for close to 1,000; tap at the buzzer for 500.
        • Streak bonus: +100 for your second right answer in a row, +200 for your third, and +300 for every one after that.
        • Wrong answers and no answer score 0 and end your streak. There's no penalty for guessing, so always tap something!

        4. Between questions

        • Everyone sees the right answer, how many picked each one, and the leaderboard.
        • The host taps Next question when the room is ready. The question also closes early once everyone has answered.

        5. Winning

        • Most points after the last question wins.
        Have a TV? Tap "Show it on a TV or laptop" in the lobby. The TV shows the question, the timer and the leaderboard big for the room.
