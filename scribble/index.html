How to play

  • Everyone writes a short phrase to start their own chain.
  • Your phone passes it on: the next person draws it, the next guesses the drawing, and so on.
  • Then each person reveals their own chain, one step at a time. That's the fun part.
  • Vote for your favorite drawing or guess. Most votes after all the rounds wins.

          How to play Scribble Telephone

          Like the old telephone game, but with drawings. The worse the drawing, the better the ending.

          1. Join

          • The host taps Host a new game and reads out the code. Everyone joins on their own phone.
          • The host picks how long to draw and how many rounds (1 to 3), then starts. You need at least 3 players.

          2. Write

          • Everyone writes a short phrase to start their own chain, like "Grandma winning at football."
          • Stuck? Tap Give me an idea.

          3. Draw, guess, repeat

          • Your phone shows you someone else's chain. If it's a phrase, draw it. If it's a drawing, guess what it is.
          • You only ever see the step right before yours.
          • No letters or numbers in drawings. That's cheating!
          • When time runs out, whatever you have gets passed on.

          4. The reveal

          • When every chain has gone around, each person reveals their own chain. Your phone gets a button; tap it to show each step on every phone and the TV.
          • Tell everyone what you wrote and what you were thinking. When you finish, it passes to the next person.

          5. Vote and score

          • After the reveals, everyone taps their favorite drawing or guess (not your own). Each vote is a point for whoever made it.
          • Then a new round starts with fresh phrases. After the last round, the most points wins.
          Have a TV? Tap "Show it on a TV or laptop" in the lobby so everyone can watch the reveal together.
