How to play

  1. One person taps Host and reads out the code. Everyone else joins with it. You need 3 to 12 players.
  2. Hold your card. Everyone sees the same secret location and their own role, except the Mole, who doesn't know where you are.
  3. Take turns asking each other questions about the location. Whoever answers asks the next question.
  4. Think you know the Mole? The host calls a vote. Mole, figured out the location? Tap the Mole button and guess.

        How to play Inside Job

        Everyone is at the same secret location except one person, the Mole. Catch the Mole before they figure out where you are.

        1. Join

        • The host taps Host a new game and reads the 4-letter code out loud. You need 3 to 12 players.
        • Everyone else enters their name and the code. The host picks a round timer, then starts.

        2. Check your card

        • Press and hold your card so nobody else can see it.
        • The crew sees the location and a role, like Bowling Alley: Shoe Rental Clerk. Roles are just for fun and for answering in character.
        • The Mole only sees "You're the Mole."
        • Everyone sees the full list of possible locations. Tap to cross them out as you go.

        3. Ask questions

        • The person shown on screen asks anyone a question about the location, like "How did you get here today?"
        • Whoever answers asks the next question, but not right back to the person who just asked them.
        • Too obvious, and the Mole figures out the location. Too vague, and people think you're the Mole.

        4. Vote

        • When time's up, or when the table is ready, the host taps Time to vote.
        • Everyone votes on their own phone. Most votes gets accused.
        • Tie? One re-vote between just the tied players. Tied again, and the Mole escapes.

        5. The Mole's move

        • Before the vote, the Mole can tap I'm the Mole and guess the location. Right, and the Mole wins. Wrong, and the crew wins.
        • The button is on every phone so it doesn't give anyone away.

        6. Who wins

        • Mole caught: the crew wins, and everyone but the Mole gets 1 point.
        • Wrong person accused, a tie that doesn't break, or a right guess: the Mole wins 2 points.
        Good questions: "What are you wearing?", "Would you bring a kid here?", "What's that smell?", "How long have you been here?"
